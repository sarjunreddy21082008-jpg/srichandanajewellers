Our Founder

Sri Ananda Rao

Sri Chandana Jewellers opened its doors on 30 November 2007 on Palakonda Road, Parvathipuram — near the Sri Vasavi Kanyaka Parameshwari Temple.

Started by Sri Ananda Rao with a simple belief — that every family deserves honest gold at a fair price — the store has become a place where Parvathipuram families come for wedding jewellery, festive gold and everyday pieces.

  • BIS hallmarked gold with a clear price breakup
  • Monthly gold savings schemes from ₹500
  • Personal attention for every customer
