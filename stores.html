    Our Story

    19 Years of Honest Gold

    Sri Chandana Jewellers was founded by Sri Ananda Rao on 30 November 2007 on Parvathipuram – Palakonda Road, near the Sri Vasavi Kanyaka Parameshwari Temple. From the first day, the idea was simple: give every family honest gold at a fair price, with the care of a family-run store.

    Today our showroom brings together traditional temple and bridal jewellery, everyday gold and diamond pieces — every piece BIS hallmarked, every bill with a full price breakup, and every customer treated like family.

    Help

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is the price of gold jewellery calculated?

    Price = (gold weight × today's rate for that purity) + making charges + stone value (if any) + 3% GST. Every product page and every bill shows this breakup.

    What is HUID hallmarking?

    HUID is a 6-digit alphanumeric code laser-marked on every hallmarked piece by the Bureau of Indian Standards. You can verify it on the BIS CARE app.

    Can I reserve a piece online and pay at the store?

    Yes. Choose "Pay at store" at checkout or message us on WhatsApp, and we will keep the piece ready for you.

    How do I join a gold savings scheme?

    Visit the showroom with your Aadhaar or PAN card, a passport-size photo and your mobile number. Plans start from ₹500 a month for 11 months. See the Gold Schemes page for details.
